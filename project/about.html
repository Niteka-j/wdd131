About Me

Helping People Build Businesses That Create Value

I am Jolis BIGIRIMANA, a Business Coach, Agripreneurship Trainer, and Social Entrepreneur passionate about entrepreneurship, agriculture, youth empowerment, and sustainable development.

For me, entrepreneurship is not simply about starting a business. It is about identifying opportunities, solving problems, creating value, and building sustainable livelihoods.

My work focuses on helping entrepreneurs and agricultural actors develop the knowledge, skills, strategies, and confidence they need to grow their businesses.

My Professional Focus

My experience includes supporting:

Entrepreneurs and young agripreneurs

Farmers and agricultural businesses

Cooperatives and producer groups

Micro, small and medium enterprises

Youth economic groups

Organizations implementing entrepreneurship and economic-development programs

My approach combines coaching, training, mentorship, technical support, and practical business tools.

My Areas of Expertise

Core competencies driving success across sectors

Entrepreneurship Development

Helping entrepreneurs move from business ideas to practical and sustainable enterprises.

Agripreneurship

Promoting agriculture as a business opportunity through market-oriented approaches and value creation.

Business Coaching

Helping entrepreneurs identify challenges, make strategic decisions, and improve their businesses.

Training & Capacity Building

Designing and delivering practical learning experiences for entrepreneurs, youth, cooperatives, and organizations.

Social Entrepreneurship

Supporting business approaches that combine economic opportunity with positive social impact.

My Mission

To empower entrepreneurs and communities with practical knowledge, tools, and support to create sustainable economic opportunities.

I believe that entrepreneurship can contribute to employment, innovation, food-system transformation, and inclusive economic development.

My Vision

A future where entrepreneurs have the knowledge, opportunities, and support they need to build businesses that are profitable, sustainable, inclusive, and impactful.
