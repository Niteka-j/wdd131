Site Planning Document

Site Name

Name: Bijos

Reasoning: Business coaching and agripreneurship are closely connected to my professional experience and interests. This website will allow me to share my knowledge, showcase my experience and projects, connect with potential clients and partners, and build my professional personal brand as a Business Coach and Agripreneurship Trainer.

Potential Domain Name: www.bijos.bi

Site Purpose

The website will present information about my professional profile, business coaching services, agripreneurship training, mentorship, and experience working with entrepreneurs and agricultural businesses.

Target Audience Scenarios

I work with people and organizations seeking to develop entrepreneurial capacity and sustainable businesses, including:

  • Young Entrepreneurs: Supporting young people to develop entrepreneurial skills and turn ideas into opportunities.
  • Agripreneurs & Farmers: Helping agricultural entrepreneurs improve their businesses, markets, and value creation.
  • Cooperatives & MPMEs: Supporting groups and small businesses to strengthen their management and business models.
  • Organizations & Development Programs: Providing coaching, training, mentorship, and technical support for entrepreneurship and economic empowerment programs.

Color Schema

Typography

Headings: Poppins (Bold, modern and entrepreneurial)

For example: "Turning Ideas into Sustainable Businesses"

Body text: Open Sans (Clean and highly readable)

For example: "Practical coaching and agripreneurship support for entrepreneurs, farmers and organizations."
